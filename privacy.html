Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

rclone-drive is used only by its owner to access their own Google Drive through rclone. It accesses only that account's Drive files, solely to copy and sync them between their own computers and servers. No data is collected from, sold to, shared with or shown to any third party, and no other users are served.

Google user data is used in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
